Key signals we look for

Artificial Urgency

Phrases like "Account suspended", "Action required within 24 hours", or "Final warning" designed to induce panic.

Credential Traps

Links asking you to "Verify your identity", "Reset password", or "Confirm billing information".

Header Impersonation

Emails displaying reputable brand names while routed through unauthorized external domains.

Deceptive Links & Lookalike Domains

Direct numeric IP addresses, uncommon web extensions (.xyz, .top), homoglyphs, or domains mimicking reputable brands.
