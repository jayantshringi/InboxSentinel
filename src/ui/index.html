How InboxSentinel analyzes messages

InboxSentinel combines machine learning with natural language heuristic inspection to recognize dangerous patterns commonly used by attackers.

Lightweight and private

🔍
Heuristic indicator extraction

In addition to raw probability scores, the scanner highlights manipulative phrasing such as artificial deadlines, spoofed verification forms, and suspicious link destinations.

Key signals we look for

Artificial Urgency

Phrases like "Account suspended", "Action required within 24 hours", or "Final warning" designed to induce panic.

Credential Traps

Links asking you to "Verify your identity", "Reset password", or "Confirm billing information".

Too Good to Be True

Unsolicited lottery winnings, 1000% crypto returns, or inheritance claims.

Deceptive Links

Direct numeric IP addresses, uncommon web extensions (.xyz, .top), or URLs mimicking reputable brands.
